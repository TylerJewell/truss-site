Why builders choose us

Late deliveries and design errors stall framing crews.

A late truss stalls a framing crew. A design error becomes a field fix and a backcharge. The company has avoided both for forty years.

Promise 01 — Delivery

Delivered and set on the date.

We run our own fleet, including crane trucks and pilot cars for oversized loads. We deliver across Oregon and Washington and set roof trusses on the plate line. If a delivery date changes, you hear it from us before it reaches your crew.

Promise 02 — Engineering

Engineered right, before it's cut.

Every truss is engineered to your spans and loads, then reviewed and sealed by a licensed Professional Engineer before it is cut. Designs meet IBC/IRC and ANSI/TPI 1. Optimized layouts reduce the lumber and connector plates each job needs.

The difference

We build components. We have since 1984.

We do not install finished buildings. We engineer, manufacture, and deliver structural components. We take the jobs we can build to spec and deliver on time.

A1

Precision engineering

Every truss is modeled to your loads and spans in Alpine's engineering software. The design sizes lumber and connector plates to meet the load and reduce waste.

A2

Stamped & code-compliant

A licensed Professional Engineer reviews and seals every project to IBC/IRC and ANSI/TPI 1. The stamped package is what your building official requires for permit and inspection.

A3

Delivered & set on time

Our own trucks, crane trucks, and pilot cars deliver across Oregon and Washington and set roof trusses on the plate line.

A4

A crew that stays

Foremen and sawyers with 15 to 30 years on the floor. Turnover is low; the same 32 people build every order.

How it works

From quote to delivery.

Sales, design, manufacturing, and delivery work from one plant in Cornelius.

STEP 01

Consult & quote

Send your plans. We review the framing, adjust where it saves material, and return an itemized quote.

STEP 02

Engineer & stamp

We model every truss to load and code, then a Professional Engineer seals the package for your building official.

STEP 03

Manufacture

Cut and plate-pressed under cover at our Cornelius plant, checked at every step.

STEP 04

Deliver & set

Our fleet delivers on the scheduled date and cranes roof trusses onto the plate line.
